## Future tests committed to

### Test 1: continuous English council by-elections (2026-05 to 2028-05)

Any council by-election in the 36 councils that have DC prior-history data may be predicted using v17.10 at pre-registration time. For each by-election:
- The methodology hash above must match exactly
- The prediction must be hashed and committed to the scorecard BEFORE polls close
- Result must be auto-scored against actual outcome
- Hit and miss are reported with equal prominence on the public scorecard

### Test 2: 2027 May UK county council + district elections (2027-05-06)

The county council election cycle in 2027 is the closest mass-prediction opportunity. v17.10 was tuned on metropolitan-borough 2026 data; **applying it to county councils tests transferability hard** — county councils have different party dynamics (more rural Con/LD vs metro Lab) and Reform-target detection was tuned on Lab→Reform metro patterns.

**Pre-registered prediction**: We expect v17.10 to score **lower** on 2027 counties than on 2026 metros — specifically:
- v17.10 score on 2027 counties (counties with DC prior data): if ≥65%, methodology generalises; if 55-65%, partial generalisation; if <55%, methodology was overfit to 2026 metro dynamics.
- v15.1 baseline on same 2027 counties: provides the comparison.

**Falsification criterion**: if v17.10 scores ≥80% on 2027 counties, the Reform-target detection generalises better than expected. If it scores ≤45%, the methodology has been falsified as a general approach (still publishable as a 2026-metro-specific finding).

### Test 3: 2028 May UK metropolitan + London borough elections (2028-05-04)

The natural in-distribution validation. Same councils as 2026 May, four years later (or two cycles for London). v17.10's expected behaviour:
- If 2028 sample size matches 2026's n=40, expected accuracy is **72.5% ± 17.5pp** (95% CI on the 2026 result).
- If v17.10 falls outside this band, the methodology either generalises differently across cycles OR the 2026 result was non-representative.

**Pre-registered prediction**: 2028 v17.10 accuracy will fall in [55%, 90%] with central tendency near 72.5%.

## Anti-hindsight commitments

1. **No methodology tweaks before the test**. The hashes above are frozen. If we want a v17.11, v18, etc., those go in as separate methodology rows — they do not replace v17.10's pre-registered scoring.
2. **No sample cherry-picking**. We commit to predicting every council in the 36-council sample that has both DC prior history AND a 2027/2028 election. If new boundary changes reduce the sample, that is reported transparently, not hidden.
3. **Wikipedia fallback only on pre-declared cases**. The 4 Wikipedia-filled councils (Camden, Croydon, Dudley, Enfield) used pre-2024 data sourced before this pre-registration; no new Wikipedia ingestion for the test councils after polls open.
4. **Confidence intervals reported**. Every accuracy claim accompanied by bootstrap 95% CIs. The published null result at n=40 acknowledged the ±17.5pp CI — future results held to the same standard.

## What this pre-registration does NOT cover

- **Per-party recall claims**: those are descriptive, computed post-hoc from any future test, and clearly framed as descriptive.
- **The Conservative/Green blind spot**: pre-registered as a known limitation. Future tests cannot "fix" this in v17.10 — only a successor methodology (v18+) can address it.
- **Cross-cycle 2024 backtest**: ruled out as infeasible (81% ward boundary review). This is documented as a methodological limitation, not a test attempt.
